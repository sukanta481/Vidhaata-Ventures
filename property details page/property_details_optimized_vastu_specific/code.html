Architectural Narrative

Emerging from the historic Alipore skyline, The Marble Villa represents a paradigm shift in urban living. Designed by award-winning curators, the structure utilizes a seamless skeleton of white Carrara marble and reinforced glass to invite the city’s light while maintaining absolute sanctuary. Every corridor is a gallery; every window, a canvas of Kolkata’s evolving heritage.
